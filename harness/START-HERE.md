# Start here

Your AI co-founder's handbook is installed. Type these in the Code tab.

- `/setup-check`  Your first-day sign-off. Green means ready. Red comes with the fix.
- `/ship`  Puts your product live on the internet, at your own address.
- `/unstuck`  Something broke? This reads the error and fixes it, or tells you what to post in Skool.
- `/update`  Gets the newest skills. Type it every morning of the bootcamp.

Stuck? Post a screenshot in the Skool setup thread.
